Style target: sober financial / corporate-business presentation visual language.

All generated backgrounds for this deck must share the SAME financial / business visual language — a consistent palette of deep navy, charcoal, slate grey, and soft ivory, with restrained muted-gold (or pale bronze) accents used sparingly for highlights. The base ground is a polished, calm, executive-boardroom feel: subtle brushed-metal sheen, soft graphite gradients, faint polished-glass reflections, and a thin pinstripe or fine grid undertone. Recurring decorative motifs may include very faint abstract line-chart silhouettes, soft candlestick / bar-chart whispers in the far margins, a thin gold rule along an edge, abstract pinstripe / grid patterns, geometric watermark wedges, and small abstract financial ticker-style horizontal accents — all kept low-contrast and unobtrusive. The overall mood is institutional, confident, and trustworthy: think Bloomberg / McKinsey / Goldman Sachs investor deck rather than a startup pitch. Avoid neon saturation, photographic imagery, hand-drawn or grunge textures, school-spirit or sports motifs, and any institutional logos, school colours, mascots, university wordmarks, monograms, campus skylines, named buildings, or other elements that could identify a specific institution (the deck must remain identity-neutral for double-blind review). The opening and closing pages can carry more decoration than the content pages without impacting foreground bbox regions, which must remain calm, low-contrast, and predominantly off-white or soft-grey so overlaid foreground text stays legible.
