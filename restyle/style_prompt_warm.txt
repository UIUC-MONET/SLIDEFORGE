Style target: warm and cozy domestic visual language.

Use rounded, hand-drawn line weights with low contrast, soft shadows, and a faint paper-grain finish over the whole image. Avoid harsh blacks, neon saturation, sharp geometric grids, and cold blue tones. The opening and closing pages can carry more decoration than the content pages without impacting foreground bbox regions, which must remain clean and low-contrast so overlaid foreground content stays legible.
